04 / What is being assumed

No fit. No forecast.

A deliberately chosen shape.

x = (K - 100) / 15
σ(K,T) = a × exp(s × x + c × x² + d × (T - 1))

a is the base σ at K = 100, T = 1. The weights control a shape, not an estimated model. Seven strikes 85..115 and five expiries 0.25..2 years make 35 synthetic calls. Rate and yield are both zero. σ is annualized in square-root-year units.

One inverse, with limits.

C = 100 × Φ(d₁) - K × Φ(d₂)
d₁ = ln(100/K)/(σ√T) + σ√T/2
d₂ = d₁ - σ√T

Invert on σ = 0..3 using bisection. Stop at bracket width ≤ 1e-10 and require price residual ≤ 1e-8. Exact lower bound means a zero-volatility limit; price 100 is an infinite-volatility limit. An interior price may exceed the 300% solver cap without violating theoretical bounds. Within 1e-8 price units above the floor, this tool declines to report an unreliable numerical σ.

Checks, not a certificate.

Vertical spreads require adjacent call-price slopes in [-1, 0]. Equal-spaced butterfly prices must be nonnegative. Calendar prices must not fall with expiry at a fixed strike here because r = q = 0. Tolerance is 1e-9 in slope or price units as appropriate.

These chosen shapes can fail those checks. Between-grid behavior, wings and full continuous arbitrage constraints are untested. No market data, bid/ask spreads, dividends, American exercise, calibration, local volatility or trading. Educational model, not investment advice.
